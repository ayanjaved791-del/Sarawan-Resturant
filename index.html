About Our Kitchen

Authentic Flavors from the Heart of Karachi

Sarawan Fast Food is proudly located in Islam Nagar Block M, Aziz Nagar, Karachi. We specialize in serving the authentic culinary tapestry that Karachi loves most: smoky charcoal BBQ, rich iron-wok Karahi, gourmet crispy Fast Food, and flavorful Chinese specialties.

Whether you crave the tender spice of our signature Chicken Tikka, the sizzling delight of a freshly prepared Chicken Karahi, or a late-night crunchy Zinger Burger, our dedicated chefs prepare every order with strict hygiene, fresh halal ingredients, and unwavering dedication to taste.

How We Cook In Aziz Nagar

Traditional Karachi food requires fire, patience, and uncompromised ingredients. Here is what happens behind our kitchen counters every single evening.

Slow-Roasted BBQ

Marinated for 12 hours in yogurt, papaya, and Shan-e-Karachi spices. Grilled slowly over smoldering mangrove coals to seal in moisture and impart that irreplaceable authentic charcoal aroma.
